HOW DO I GET A TOKEN?
  1. Go to github.com/settings/tokens and choose Generate new token (classic)
  2. Leave every scope unchecked (public data needs no scopes)
  3. Copy the ghp_… token.
  4. Safest: paste it into a .env file here as GITHUB_TOKEN=ghp_…, then restart node server.mjs. It never leaves your machine.
  5. Or: paste it here — it is stored only in your browser (localStorage).
